How this is calculated

Useful solar energy = exposure × collector area × efficiency ÷ 3.6. Storage mass = stored energy × 3,600 ÷ material capacity. Sensible capacity uses specific heat × temperature band; phase-change materials also include latent heat.

Insulation uses equivalent-cube surface area and planar conduction over the holding period. Enclosure sizing follows the workbook’s 21-step self-weight iteration. These are preliminary estimates, not fabrication-ready designs.
